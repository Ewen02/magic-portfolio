## Overview

As part of a **freelance assignment**, I designed and developed the **showcase website** for a dental practice located in **Bures-sur-Yvette** (Essonne, France). The goal: to give the practice a professional, clear, and reassuring online presence that introduces the team, the treatments offered, and makes it easy for patients to book appointments.

The site is **in production** at [cabinetdentairedesboeuf.fr](https://cabinetdentairedesboeuf.fr/).

## Business context

For a dental practice, the website plays a dual role: **reassuring** new patients (presenting the practitioners, the premises, and the treatments) and **simplifying** common tasks (booking appointments, opening hours, directions, handling emergencies). The challenge was to produce a **clear, fast, and accessible** site that inspires confidence from the very first visit and works just as well on mobile as on desktop.

## Features

- **Presentation of the practice** and the **two practitioners** (background, qualifications, insurance coverage).
- **Detailed treatments** offered: conservative dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, implantology, orthodontics, pediatric dentistry, prosthetics, emergencies.
- **Gallery** of the premises to highlight a modern and reassuring environment.
- **Patient testimonials** and **FAQ** answering the most common questions.
- **Online appointment booking** through the **Doctolib** integration, with visible, repeated CTAs.
- **Practical information**: detailed opening hours, directions (RER, bus, parking), wheelchair accessibility, Google Maps map.
- **Emergency section** with procedures and useful phone numbers.

## Architecture & approach

- **One-page site** structured into vertical sections, with anchor-based navigation for a smooth journey.
- **Mobile-first responsive design**: readability and ergonomics optimized for smartphones (adapted menu, accessible CTAs).
- **Performance**: fast loading, optimized images served in multiple formats.
- **Local SEO**: semantic content structure and markup designed to rank on local searches ("dentist Bures-sur-Yvette").
- **Accessibility**: carefully crafted contrasts, heading hierarchy, and keyboard navigation.

## Contributions

- Design of the **site structure** and mockup based on the practice's needs.
- Full **integration** of the design into a responsive, high-performance site.
- **Doctolib integration** for online appointment booking.
- Implementation of **local SEO** and structured **practical information**.
- **Deployment** and release of the site to production.

## Challenges and lessons learned

- Translating the needs of a **non-technical client** into a clear, self-sufficient site.
- Carefully crafting the **mobile experience**, which is the majority for this kind of local practice.
- Optimizing **local SEO** to maximize visibility on geolocated searches.
- Delivering a **reassuring and professional** product in a sector where trust is decisive.

## Impact

- Site **in production** and used daily by the practice and its patients.
- Smooth **online appointment booking** via Doctolib.
- **Positive** patient feedback on the clarity and modernity of the site.
